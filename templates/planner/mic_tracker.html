🎧 Listen — setup

The ShowStack Listen app runs on the wired Mac at your rack and streams beltpack audio to A2 phones over the show Wi-Fi. Audio never passes through ShowStack.

1 · Start on the rack Mac
Didn't open? ShowStack Listen isn't installed on this Mac yet . Unzip it, drag it to Applications, and open it once — that's what lets this button launch it. Then click Start again.
Not connected yet.
Opens on this Mac, while the app is running.
2 · Audio input & phones
  • Pick the input from the 🎧 menu bar icon → Audio Input — Dante Virtual Soundcard or any interface (USB preamp, MADI, AVB). A lone Dante input is picked automatically.
  • Channel numbers are that input's numbers; use Audio Ch on a card when a pack isn't patched 1:1.
  • Phones: 📱 Set up phones (above, once the app is running) shows QR codes to install the certificate once per phone and open this show on it.
