Locations are places like FOH, Monitor World or Stage Left rack. Assign gear to them, then print a sheet of what lives at each one.

Steps

  1. Open Equip Locations in the sidebar and tap Add Equip Location.
  2. Enter a Name, plus an optional Description and Sort order. Tap Save.
  3. On each console, I/O device, system processor and COMM belt pack, set its Location.
  4. To print one location, tap Export PDF in the Export Inventory column.
  5. To print every location, tap Export All Locations PDF at the top of the list. Each location starts on a new page.

Good to know

  • Amps don't use these locations. They have their own Amp Locations, so amps don't appear on location sheets.
  • The list is sorted by Sort order (lowest first), but the all-locations PDF is sorted A–Z.
  • Deleting a location also deletes every system processor assigned to it. Consoles, I/O devices and belt packs just lose their location.
