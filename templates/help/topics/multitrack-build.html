Build a named, coloured track list for your recorder from a console's channels.

Steps

  1. Open Multitrack Sessions in the sidebar and click + New Session.
  2. Enter a Name and pick the Console, Target DAW, Feed source and Track order. Recorder capacity is optional. Then click Create session.
  3. In Add Tracks, tick channels on the Inputs, Aux, Matrix and Stereo tabs. Use Manual for extras such as a click track. Then click Add … selected.
  4. Edit the list. Untick a track to leave it out of exports. Click a name to rename it. Click the swatch to set a colour. Drag ⋮⋮ to reorder, or click × to remove.
  5. To reuse this layout, click Save as Template.

Good to know

  • The picker only shows channels that have Multitrack ticked on the console.
  • Dragging a track changes Track order to Custom.
  • If you set a recorder capacity, the bar warns when the session goes over it.
  • Templates belong to your login and work in any project. They keep only ticked tracks and match them by channel number. A channel missing on the new console is skipped.
  • Changing tracks, and Import Console CSV, are for editors and owners only.
