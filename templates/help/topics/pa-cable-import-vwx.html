Bring cable runs drawn in Vectorworks into the schedule from a worksheet export.

Steps

  1. In PA Cable Entries, tap Import from VWX.
  2. Choose the CSV exported from your Vectorworks cable worksheet and tap Preview import.
  3. Check each row: new, update, error or warning. Nothing is saved yet.
  4. Tap Confirm import. A message gives the number created, updated and skipped.

How rows are read

  • Every run needs a unique Cable Label of 20 characters or fewer. Each new label becomes a zone.
  • Lengths round up to whole feet. The 20% spare is still added only in the Quick Order List.
  • A row with Extension Of becomes an extension on that run's fan-out, rounded up to 25', 50', 100' or 150'.

Importing again

  • Runs are matched by label, and runs not in the file are left alone.
  • An import updates cable type, length, destination and fan-out. A blank Destination or Fan-Out keeps what the entry has.
  • The file replaces a fan-out's extensions, including any added by hand. The preview lists what will be replaced.
  • A label on two or more entries, or on an entry linked to Soundvision / Amps, is refused.
