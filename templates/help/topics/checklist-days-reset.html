Match the daily columns to your show days, and clear the checklist when the next show starts.

Steps

  1. Open Audio Checklists in the sidebar and pick FOH or A2.
  2. In the Daily Tasks header, click + Day or − Day. You can have 1 to 14 days.
  3. To rename a day, click its heading (for example Day 1), type a name such as "Rehearsal" and press Enter.
  4. To start over, click Reset All and confirm.

Good to know

  • FOH and A2 each keep their own number of days. If you remove a day, its statuses are kept and come back when you add the day again.
  • Day names are stored only in this browser. Other crew, and your other devices, still see Day 1, Day 2 and so on.
  • Reset All clears all three lists. It deletes every task, including the ones you added, and puts the starter tasks back. All statuses go back to Not Started and each list returns to 4 days. This can't be undone.
  • To keep your own tasks through a reset, save a template first and load it again afterwards.
