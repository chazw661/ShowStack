Turns the day's plan into Mic Tracker sessions: one per block between breaks, one row per RF, its wearers in order.

Steps

  1. On the Mic Plan page, press Send to Mic Tracker. It is off while the plan is out of date or has an RF conflict.
  2. Read the preview. Nothing is written yet.
  3. Under Changed in Mic Tracker since the last push, tick Keep Mic Tracker's for changes made on purpose.
  4. Press Send to Mic Tracker, then reprint the labels it lists.

Good to know

  • RF numbers are the plan's packs, gaps and all; never renumbered.
  • Hosts keep one RF, with a row only in sessions they're on stage. Backups and the spare get their own rows.
  • Sending again updates the same slots. Photos, notes, settings, groups, MIC'D marks and the A2's mic type stay, and move with the slot.
  • A slot with a photo, notes or MIC'D is never removed.
  • A session with a mic MIC'D is skipped unless you tick Include started sessions.
  • Revert last push undoes it. It stops if Mic Tracker changed since, unless you tick Revert anyway.
