The Wiring Diagram draws the system, stage box to speakers. It changes nothing.

Steps

  1. Open Wiring Diagram in the sidebar, under PA Cable Entries.
  2. Pick a tab: Overview, Dante (with IPs), Analogue/AES or Speaker cables.
  3. Read left to right. A label shows its ports (In 1–15 → Ch 1–15); lines into amps and processors end on a named input (XLR 1). A PA run shows its cable.
  4. Set each I/O device's Console connection on its page. A banner lists devices without one.
  5. Check Warnings under the diagram; fix them on their pages.

Good to know

  • Solid lines are links you made; dashed ones are matched from labels.
  • Orange "?" lines lead to text naming no device, or several.
  • Green dotted lines are AVB and Dante network feeds. Show network feeds (Overview) hides them; slots still name the source.
  • A label too long to draw shows a key, [1], listed under Connections.
  • Grey dotted lines are console links with no Console connection set.
  • Console connection is per device: one on TWINLANe to one console and Dante to another can't be shown yet.
  • An amp input naming no output is under Unlinked feeds.
