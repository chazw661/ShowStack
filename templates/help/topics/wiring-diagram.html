The Wiring Diagram draws the system, stage box to speakers. It reads your pages and changes nothing.

Steps

  1. Open Wiring Diagram in the sidebar, under PA Cable Entries.
  2. Read left to right.
  3. A box names its ports: outputs on the right (Out 17), inputs on the left (XLR 1 ← Y001 Out 3). A line's label names its far end (SL P1 AES 1 · AES). A PA run shows its cable.
  4. Show at 100% shows it full size; Fit to width goes back.
  5. Fix any Warnings on their pages, then reload.
  6. Download gives a PDF (11×17 or 24×36) or a DXF per tab for Vectorworks. Tick Network feeds to include them. An Overview too big for one page prints as three: inputs to console to outputs, outputs to processors to amps, amps to speakers. A device on the boundary is on both pages.

Good to know

  • Solid lines are links you picked on a page. Dashed lines are matched from labels.
  • Orange "?" lines lead to text naming no device, or several.
  • Green dotted lines are AVB and Dante network feeds, with no cable.
  • Faded lines are B inputs, labelled (B).
  • Amp input text naming no output is a box under Unlinked feeds.
  • Amps are grouped by amp location; uncabled arrays sit under Not cabled.
  • Devices sharing a name, location and IP are listed in Warnings: rename one.
