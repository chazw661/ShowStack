The Wiring Diagram draws the whole system on one page, from stage-box inputs to speakers. It reads what you entered on the patch, processor, amp and PA cable pages. Anyone on the project can open it; it changes nothing.

Steps

  1. Open Wiring Diagram in the sidebar, under PA Cable Entries.
  2. Read left to right: I/O inputs, console, I/O outputs, processors, amps, speakers. Each I/O device has an inputs box and an outputs box.
  3. A line's label shows its ports (In 1–15 → Ch 1–15). A PA cable run also shows its cable label, count, type and length.
  4. Show at 100% shows it full size to scroll around; Fit to width goes back.
  5. Check Warnings under the diagram. Fix the label or link on its own page, then reload.

Good to know

  • Solid lines are links you made on a page.
  • Dashed lines are matched from labels and follow any label change.
  • Orange lines with "?" lead to text that names no device, or more than one.
  • Green dotted lines are AVB network feeds, with no cable.
  • Amps are grouped by amp location.
