The Wiring Diagram draws the whole system, from stage-box inputs to speakers. It reads your pages and changes nothing.

Steps

  1. Open Wiring Diagram in the sidebar, under PA Cable Entries.
  2. Pick a tab: Overview, Dante (with IPs), Analog signal flow or Speaker cables.
  3. Read left to right. A label shows its ports (In 1–15 → Ch 1–15); lines into amps and processors end on a named input (XLR 1). A PA run shows its cable.
  4. Set each I/O device's Console connection (Dante, MADI…) on its page. A banner lists devices without one.
  5. Check Warnings under the diagram. Fix it on its page, then reload.

Good to know

  • Solid lines are links you made; dashed ones are matched from labels.
  • Orange lines with "?" lead to text naming no device, or several.
  • Green dotted lines are AVB and Dante network feeds, with no cable.
  • Grey dotted lines are console links with no Console connection set.
  • Console connection is per device: one on TWINLANe to one console and Dante to another can't be shown yet.
  • An amp input naming no output is under Unlinked feeds.
