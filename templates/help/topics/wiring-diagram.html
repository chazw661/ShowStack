The Wiring Diagram draws the system, stage box to speakers. It reads your pages and changes nothing.

Steps

  1. Open Wiring Diagram in the sidebar, under PA Cable Entries.
  2. Read left to right: device inputs, console, device outputs, processors, amps, speakers.
  3. A label shows its ports (In 1–15 → Ch 1–15). Lines into amps and processors end on a named input (XLR 1, AES 2). A PA run shows its cable.
  4. Show at 100% shows it full size; Fit to width goes back.
  5. Check Warnings under the diagram; fix them on their pages, then reload.

Good to know

  • Solid lines are links you picked on a page (Out 17 · AES). Dashed lines are matched from labels and follow any label change.
  • Orange "?" lines lead to text naming no device, or several.
  • Green dotted lines are AVB and Dante network feeds, with no cable.
  • An amp input naming no output is a box under Unlinked feeds, with the reason.
  • Amps are grouped by amp location. Arrays with no cable sit under Not cabled.
  • Two devices with the same name, location and IP are listed in Warnings: rename one so its outputs can be linked.
