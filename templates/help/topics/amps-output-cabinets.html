Each amp card has a Cabinets per output strip. Say what is plugged into each output and ShowStack shows the load the amp sees.

Steps

  1. Open Amplifier Assignments and find the amp.
  2. In Cabinets per output, pick the section on an output, for example Kara II · LF. Typing the first letters jumps to one. — none — clears it.
  3. Use − and + to set how many are in parallel.
  4. Set the Tag, such as LF_B. The zone comes from the cabinet; it saves once you pick a slot.
  5. Read the line underneath, for example Kara II · LF ×3 → 2.67 Ω (2.7 Ω). That's the impedance divided by the number in parallel; the bracket is the nearest published load.

Good to know

  • No cabinet means nothing is assigned yet.
  • Below … minimum means the load is lower than one output is rated for: 2.7 Ω on LA12X and LA8, 4 Ω on LA4X and LA7.16i (L-Acoustics Preset Guide).
  • No power figures for this model means the amp isn't counted in power estimates yet.
  • Cabinets and tags print on the amplifier PDF and the Complete System Report. Viewers can't change them.
