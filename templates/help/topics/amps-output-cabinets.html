Say what is plugged into each amp output and ShowStack shows the load the amp sees.

Steps

  1. Open Amplifier Assignments and find the amp.
  2. In Cabinets per output, pick the section on an output, for example Kara II · LF. Typing the first letters jumps to one. — none — clears it.
  3. Use − and + to set how many are in parallel.
  4. Read the line underneath, for example Kara II · LF ×3 → 2.67 Ω (2.7 Ω). That's the impedance divided by the number in parallel; the bracket is the nearest published load.

Good to know

  • No cabinet means nothing is assigned yet.
  • Below … minimum means the load is under the output's rating: 2.7 Ω on LA12X and LA8, 4 Ω on LA4X and LA7.16i.
  • No power figures for this model means the amp isn't counted in power estimates yet.
  • Routing (e.g. LF_B) is set in the Ch block mid-card, not here. An amber note such as CH 1 is routed LF, cabinet is Kara II · HF means they conflict (PA never does); nothing changes on its own.
  • Cabinets and notes print on the amplifier PDF and the Complete System Report. Viewers can't change them.
