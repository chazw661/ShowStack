Each amp card has a Cabinets per output strip. Say what is plugged into each output and ShowStack shows the load the amp sees.

Steps

  1. Open Amplifier Assignments and find the amp.
  2. In Cabinets per output, pick the section on an output, for example Kara II · LF. Cabinets are grouped by type, and typing the first letters jumps to one. Pick — none — to clear it.
  3. Use − and + to set how many are in parallel.
  4. Read the line underneath, for example Kara II · LF ×3 → 2.67 Ω (2.7 Ω). The first figure is the section's impedance divided by the number in parallel. The bracket is the nearest load the amp's draw table is published for.

Good to know

  • No cabinet means nothing is assigned yet.
  • Below … minimum means the load is lower than one output is rated for: 2.7 Ω on LA12X and LA8, 4 Ω on LA4X and LA7.16i (L-Acoustics Preset Guide).
  • No power figures for this model means the amp isn't counted in power estimates yet.
  • The cabinets also print on the amplifier PDF and the Complete System Report.
  • Viewers can see them but not change them.
