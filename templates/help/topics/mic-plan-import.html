Mic Plan reads a show day's run-of-show PDF and lists who goes on stage, when they are sent and caught, and anything that does not add up. It does not change the Mic Tracker.

Steps

  1. Open Mic Plan in the sidebar and pick the Day.
  2. Choose the PDF and press Import. If the day already has a part covering the same times, you are asked whether to Replace it or Add as a new part.
  3. Check Crew names: cues are read as "<crew> sends / catches <names>". Add a missing name and press Save & re-read.
  4. In People, merge a nickname into the right person (a set of initials into the full name, say), link a Presenter and set a mic hint.
  5. Work through Flags. Each one links to its line.

Good to know

  • Editors and owners only; viewers can read the page.
  • The PDF is read and thrown away. Only its rows are kept.
  • Merges, links and hints belong to the day and survive a re-import.
  • A pickle is a clicker, not a mic.
