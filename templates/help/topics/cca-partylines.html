Set up the talk channels, such as Production, Audio and Lighting, that every beltpack key and port is routed to.

Steps

  1. Open a config in COMM Config. The Partylines tab opens first.
  2. Click a channel card's name, type a new one (e.g. "Show Call") and press Enter. It saves when you leave the field.
  3. Click + Add Partyline for another channel. It takes the next number and a PL name you can rename.
  4. Click ✕ on a card to delete that partyline.

Good to know

  • Keep names to 20 characters or fewer.
  • A new name shows up straight away in every key and port dropdown.
  • Tick HelixNet on a partyline to offer it to HelixNet roles. HelixNet beltpacks, stations and remotes only list ticked partylines.
  • Deleting a partyline leaves any key or port that used it — Unassigned —.
  • FreeSpeak II: keep to twelve partylines. The export only renames the base station's twelve channels. A partyline you add past twelve isn't created in the file, and any key or port routed to it points at a channel the base station doesn't have.
