Get a printed sheet of every beltpack, with who has it, where it lives and what its keys are, to leave at the comms rack.

Steps

  1. Go to the System Overview page (Home in the breadcrumb trail).
  2. Click System PDF. The report opens in a new tab.
  3. Scroll to the COMM System section and print the pages you need from the browser.

Good to know

  • The COMM section lists wireless and hardwired devices separately, with BP #, Position, Name, Location, Headset, CH 1–CH 4 and IP.
  • Only the first four channels are printed. A pack with more keys is cut short on paper.
  • For a sheet per rack or position, open Equip Locations and click Export PDF on one location or Export All Locations PDF. It lists the comm devices at each location along with the other gear there.
  • The report shows the project as it is now. Reprint after any changes.
