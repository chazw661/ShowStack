Several people can work in the Mic Tracker at once, for example the A1 at FOH and two A2s backstage. This is how each screen picks up the others' changes.

Steps

  1. On the Tracker tab, just keep working. Every few seconds the page checks for changes and updates names, MIC'D, mic settings, notes and photos in place. There is nothing to press.
  2. Watch the pill at the bottom right: Saving…, then Saved ✓. If it says Save failed, reload the page and make the change again.
  3. On the Overview tab, a banner reads The show was updated — refresh to see the latest. Press 🔄 Refresh Now, or Dismiss to finish what you are doing first.
  4. On a session's settings page an Updates available! banner offers Refresh Now. If you haven't touched the page for 30 seconds, it reloads on its own.

Good to know

  • New days, new sessions and presenters added to a shared mic on another screen show up only after you reload.
  • A tab in the background catches up as soon as you switch back to it.
  • Everyone must have the same project selected.
