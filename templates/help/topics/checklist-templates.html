Save the task list you like and load it into your next show, so you don't rebuild it every time.

Steps

  1. Open Audio Checklists in the sidebar and set up the tasks the way you want across FOH, A2 and Prep.
  2. Click Save as Template, enter a Template Name and click Save Template.
  3. In the next project, open the checklist and click Load Template.
  4. Click Load next to the template you want, then confirm.
  5. To remove a template, click 🗑 next to it in the Load Template list.

Good to know

  • Loading replaces ALL current tasks in all three lists. Statuses start again at Not Started. The number of days stays as it is.
  • A template keeps each task's name, its list, whether it is a setup or daily task, and its stage. It doesn't keep statuses or day names.
  • Templates belong to your login. You can use them in any of your projects, but other crew can't see them.
  • If you save under a name you already use, the old template is replaced without a warning.
  • Only editors and owners can load a template. Viewers get Read-only access.
