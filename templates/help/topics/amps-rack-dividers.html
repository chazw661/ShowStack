Break a long rack location into labelled sections, such as "Mains" and "Subs", and put your locations in order.

Steps

  1. In Amplifier Assignments, tap + Add Divider on a location's add row. The divider appears below the last amp.
  2. Type a label in the — divider label — field. It saves when you press Enter or leave the field.
  3. Use ▲ and ▼ on the divider to move it up or down one amp at a time.
  4. ✕ deletes the divider after you confirm.
  5. To reorder whole locations, tap Locations and use ▲ and ▼. Type over a name to rename it.

Good to know

  • You can't reorder amps inside a location on this page. Each new amp goes to the bottom, so add them in rack order.
  • Dividers don't print. The amp PDF groups amps by location only.
  • Tap a location header to fold it, or use Collapse / Expand All.
