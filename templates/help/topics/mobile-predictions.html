Walk the room with the PA prediction on your phone: trim, weight and box angles for every array.

Steps

  1. On your phone, sign in at /m/ and open your project.
  2. Tap Predictions, then a prediction file.
  3. Tap an array to see Weight, Trim, Bumper or Hole, Azimuth, MBar and Config. Tap Show Cabinets for each box's model, angle and Panflex.

Good to know

  • Use Expand All and Collapse All to open or close every array at once.
  • Predictions are read-only on the phone. Upload Soundvision PDFs in the full site.
