Share one link with the whole crew. Each person who opens it asks for access, and you approve each request as an editor or a viewer.

Steps

  1. On the Project Dashboard, tap Manage Team on your project, then Admin Panel. No Admin Panel button? Invite people by email instead.
  2. Under Access & Sharing, tap Copy next to Shareable Access Request Link. Paste it into a text or email to your crew.
  3. Each person opens the link and signs in. Anyone without an account creates one, then opens the link again. They can add a note, then tap Request Access to followed by your project's name.
  4. You get an email for each request. Tap Review Request in that email to open the Access Requests page.
  5. Pick Viewer or Editor and tap Approve, or tap Deny. Approved people get an email too.

Good to know

  • Nobody gets in until you approve them. Anyone you deny can send a new request.
  • The link starts with showstack-production.up.railway.app, not showstack.io. It still opens ShowStack.
  • The link doesn't expire and is the same for everyone, so only share it with your crew.
  • Requests you've already handled are listed under Reviewed.
