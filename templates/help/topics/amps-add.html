Build your amp racks: make a location for each rack position, then add the amps that live there.

Steps

  1. Open Amplifier Assignments in the sidebar.
  2. No locations yet? Tap Locations, type a name (e.g. "SL Racks") and tap + Add.
  3. Under the location, on the + Add Amp: row, choose the amp in Pick amp model…, type a name (e.g. "SL LA12X 1") and tap Add.
  4. The page reloads with the new amp card at the bottom of that location, one row per amp channel.
  5. Fill in IP Address and Preset on the card. Each field saves when you leave it.

Good to know

  • An amp needs both a model and a name.
  • Deep edit opens the full form: location, model, colour and connector labels. Change the location there to move an amp to another rack.
  • Switching an amp to a model with fewer channels deletes the extra channels and their labels.
  • × on a card deletes the amp. You can't undo it.
  • A location that still has amps can't be deleted. Move or delete its amps first.
  • The search box and location chips help you find an amp.
