Print a label for the front of each bodypack showing whose pack it is.

Steps

  1. In a session's A2 tab press 🖨 Print labels.
  2. Choose One session, One day or Whole show.
  3. Choose Single name (one label per presenter) or Running order (one label per pack listing its presenters; per day for a whole show).
  4. Pick colour or black & white, and the first unused label under Start at label.
  5. Check the preview, press ↓ Download PDF and print at 100% / Actual size, never Fit to page.

Lining up

  1. Print the alignment page on plain paper. Both bars should measure exactly 100 mm.
  2. Hold it over a label sheet against a window and enter any offset under Printer calibration (+X right, +Y down). This browser remembers it.

Good to know

  • Sized for OnlineLabels OL3016LP, 2" square, 12 per sheet.
  • A presenter on one pack for consecutive sessions gets one label or line, marked "+2" for the extra sessions.
  • Empty slots are skipped. Viewers can print too.
