Let an A2 hear one beltpack on their phone to check they have the right pack and to catch lav rub or headset rattle.

Steps

  1. With Listen running on the rack Mac, press 📱 Set up phones (QR codes) in Listen Setup, or Set up a phone… in the 🎧 menu.
  2. On the phone, scan Install the certificate. Install the profile in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, then turn on ShowStack Listen in Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings. Do this once per phone.
  3. Scan Open Listen. No ShowStack sign-in is needed.
  4. Pick the Channel (and the Session if asked), then press ▶︎ Listen. Adjust Boost as needed.
  5. If the A2 is also signed in to ShowStack on the phone, 🎧 Listen on any A2 card opens that pack directly.

Good to know

  • The channel is the RF number. If a pack isn't patched 1:1, enter its input number in Audio Ch on the card (editors and owners only).
  • The phone must be on a network that can reach the rack Mac. Guest Wi-Fi that isolates its users will block it.
  • If the phone stays silent, press 🔊 Tap for sound.
