Each person on a project is the owner, an editor or a viewer. The owner can do everything. Editors can add, edit and delete show data. Viewers can look but not change anything.

Steps

  1. To change someone's role: on the Project Dashboard, tap Manage Team on your project. Pick Editor — can view and edit or Viewer — can only view next to their name and tap Save.
  2. To leave a project someone shared with you: under Projects Shared With You, tap Leave Project and confirm.
  3. To delete a project you own: tap Delete Project on its card and confirm. This permanently deletes all of its show data.

Good to know

  • Only the owner can invite people, approve access requests, change roles or delete the project. Editors can't do any of these.
  • Editors can add new equipment as well as edit what's already there.
  • Roles apply to one project at a time, so you can be an editor on one show and a viewer on another.
  • Manage Team has no button for removing a member. Members can leave on their own.
  • Creating your own projects needs a paid or beta account. Free accounts can join projects.
