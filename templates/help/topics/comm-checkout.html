Track which wireless beltpacks have gone out to crew and which are still on the charger.

Steps

  1. Open Comm Devices in the sidebar.
  2. In the Checked out column, tap IN to check a pack out. It turns to OUT and saves straight away.
  3. Tap OUT to check it back in.
  4. To do a batch, tick the devices, choose Check out selected belt packs (Wireless only) or Check in selected belt packs from the action menu, and click Go.

Good to know

  • The Wireless card at the top keeps a live Total, Out and Avail count.
  • Only wireless devices check out. Hardwired devices show a dash, because they are cabled to the system.
  • Use the Checked out filter above the list to see only the packs still out at load-out.
  • Viewers can see the buttons, but a tap does not change anything.
  • On the phone COMM page, tap a device's row to flip it between IN and OUT.
