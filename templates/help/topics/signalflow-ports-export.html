Label I/O, connect the gear and export a picture for the show file.

Steps

  1. Click a shape. Under Ports, click + Add port on Top, Bottom, Left or Right and type a label. 🗑 removes a port.
  2. Drag a teal corner handle to resize the selected shape.
  3. Hover over a shape to show its port dots, then drag from one dot to a dot on another shape. If a shape has no labelled ports, drag from its right or bottom dot.
  4. Click the connector and set Signal type, Direction and Circuit label. Drag the line to reroute it.
  5. For a note, click T, click the canvas, type and press Enter. For an area, click the boundary tool, click each corner, then press Enter.
  6. Click PNG (or PDF).

Good to know

  • Everything saves on its own. If Save failed — retry appears, click it.
  • If someone else edits it at the same time, click Reload on the banner.
  • Adding the first port replaces the four default dots. When you remove a port, its connectors stay on the shape.
  • The PNG is cropped to your drawing on a white background. The PDF fits on one letter page.
