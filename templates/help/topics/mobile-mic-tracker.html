A phone-sized, read-only view of who is on which mic, for quick checks backstage.

Steps

  1. On your phone, go to /m/ on the ShowStack site and sign in with your Username and Password.
  2. Pick your project under My Projects or Shared With Me.
  3. Tap Mic Tracker, then a day, then a session.
  4. Scroll the mic list, or use Search presenter or mic #... to find someone.

Good to know

  • Each mic shows its RF number, mic type, a D-MIC badge when set, the presenter and any A1 notes.
  • MIC'D can't be marked here. Mark it in the full Mic Tracker. The phone reminds you: Remember to update ShowStack desktop after micing presenters.
  • On a mic shared by several presenters, only the presenter who is currently up is shown.
  • Days and sessions are added in the full Mic Tracker. They then appear here.
  • Keep me signed in for 30 days is ticked by default. Untick it and you stay signed in for 7 days.
  • Editors and owners also get an Edit in Admin button on the project screen.
