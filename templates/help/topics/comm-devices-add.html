Log every beltpack, key panel and speaker station on the show: its number, who wears it, where it lives and which channels it carries.

Steps

  1. Open Comm Devices in the sidebar.
  2. For one device, click Add Comm Device. Pick the Model and enter the Device #, then fill Position, Name and Headset Type.
  3. Under Channels, add a row per key: Channel # and its Assignment. Click Save.
  4. For a batch, click Add multiple. Twenty rows appear with Device #s continuing from your highest. Fill Model, Position, Name, Headset and Group, then click Create devices. Rows you leave blank are skipped.

Good to know

  • Wireless or hardwired comes from the Model. A hardwired device shows IP Address; a wireless one shows Checked out.
  • Position and Name are pick lists. Fill them first with the Positions and Crew Names buttons; channel names live under Channels.
  • Two devices sharing a Device # get a warning, but the save still goes through.
  • Missing a model from the list? Only a ShowStack administrator can add one.
  • Add multiple is for editors and owners only.
