Print the beltpack labels in the client's colours, with their logo.

Steps

  1. On Print labels, press Edit branding (owners and editors).
  2. Set the background colour and up to three accents, or press Suggest from logo and check them. Choose angled corner bars or an edge stripe, then Save colours.
  3. Upload PNG or JPEG logos: one for dark backgrounds, one for light, and an icon-only mark.
  4. Check the preview in colour and black & white.

Good to know

  • Text turns white or black, whichever reads better on the background.
  • The logo prints top-right, 8 mm high, or smaller to fit, down to 6 mm. Wider still, and the mark prints instead.
  • Corner bars cost one name on a full running order; the edge stripe costs none.
  • Print the alignment page first: its swatches show the venue printer's colour.
  • Dark labels use a lot of toner. Black & white prints them in grey.
