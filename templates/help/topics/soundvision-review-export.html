Check every array from the prediction, then take the numbers to the rigger as a spreadsheet or a PDF.

Steps

  1. In Soundvision Predictions, tap View Details on the prediction.
  2. The header shows Version, Generated, Total Arrays and Total Weight.
  3. Arrays are grouped by Soundvision group. Tap a group or array name to open it, or use Expand All.
  4. Each array lists Bumper, Motors, Total Weight, Bottom Trim, Site Angle, Azimuth, pickups and motor loads. Below that is a box-by-box table: Model, Angle, Site, Top Z, Bottom Z and Panflex.
  5. Tap Export CSV for a spreadsheet with one row per array.
  6. For a PDF, open the prediction from the Soundvision Predictions list and tap Export PDF at the bottom.

Good to know

  • The badges on an array name (weight, trim, boxes, MBar) come from the first array of that name. Open it to see each one, e.g. left and right.
  • To fix a value, edit it in the arrays section of the prediction's edit page.
