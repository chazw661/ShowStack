Print every amp card as a rack sheet for the system tech or to tape inside the rack door.

Steps

  1. Open Amplifier Assignments.
  2. Tap Export to PDF at the top of the page.
  3. Your device downloads Amplifier_Assignments.pdf. It is landscape, on US Letter paper.

Good to know

  • Amps are grouped under their location, in the same location order as the page.
  • Each amp is one card with its inputs, IP address, preset, channel settings and outputs. A card is never split across two pages.
  • Only channels that have a setting picked show up in the card's settings table.
  • The PDF always has every amp in the project. The search box and location chips don't change what prints.
  • Dividers don't print.
