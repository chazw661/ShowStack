Save the people you work with as a crew, then add the whole crew to a show in one go.

Steps

  1. On the Project Dashboard, tap My Crew (in the planner it's in the menu under your name).
  2. Enter a Crew Name and tap + Create Crew.
  3. Under Add Member, enter a Username or Email, pick a Default Role, tap + Add to Crew. Repeat.
  4. Under Add to a Project, pick the Project and tap Add this crew to project. (A project's invite page also has Add this crew.)

Good to know

  • No account yet? Enter their full email. They show as pending signup and get an email asking them to sign up. When they register with that address they're added to every project this crew was added to, with their default role.
  • Members with an account get access right away, plus an email.
  • People already on the project are skipped; their role isn't changed.
  • Removing someone from a crew, or deleting the crew, doesn't remove project access they already have.
  • Crews are private to you, and you can only add them to projects you own.
