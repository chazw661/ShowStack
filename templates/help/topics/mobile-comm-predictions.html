Walk the room with the PA prediction on your phone, and check comm beltpacks in and out at the comm desk.

Steps

  1. On your phone, sign in at /m/ and open your project.
  2. Tap Predictions, then a prediction file.
  3. Tap an array to see Weight, Trim, Bumper or Hole, Azimuth, MBar and Config. Tap Show Cabinets for each box's model, angle and Panflex.
  4. On the COMM list, tap a beltpack row to switch it between IN and OUT.

Good to know

  • Use Expand All and Collapse All to open or close every array at once.
  • Predictions are read-only on the phone. Upload Soundvision PDFs in the full site.
  • The COMM list groups packs under Wireless and Hardwired, has a search box (name, position or BP #), and counts Total and Out at the bottom.
  • The project screen has no COMM tile yet, so the COMM list opens only from its own address. [CHECK]
