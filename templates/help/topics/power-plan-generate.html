Build a power plan straight from your amp racks: one row per amp, on the cabinets on its outputs, with the legs balanced.

Steps

  1. Put the amps in racks and the cabinets on their outputs.
  2. On each amp card, leave Leg on Auto, or set L1/L2/L3 where an amp must sit on a given leg.
  3. Tap Generate power plan. Auto legs are balanced; legs you set are never moved.
  4. Choose the service and programme on the plan. When amps or cabinets change, the plan says so: tap Regenerate. Your legs, settings and rows added by hand are kept.

Good to know

  • The legs follow the service: L1–L3 on 3-phase, L1–L2 on split-phase, L1 on single-phase.
  • An amp with no cabinets is counted at its worst case, and the row says so.
  • Other audio electronics (consoles, processors, playback) is one allowance, 2000 W by default, spread evenly across the legs.
  • Every amp except the LA4X needs 3-phase distro. On split- or single-phase the plan warns; it doesn't stop you.
