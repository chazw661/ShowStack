Add the breakouts, extensions and couplers on a cable run, and log short box-to-box links as jumpers.

Steps

  1. In PA Cable Entries, open the run.
  2. Under PA Fan Outs, pick a Fan out type (e.g. NL4 Y, NL8 to 2XNL4, DOSub) and a Quantity.
  3. Tap Save. The "Cable saved." banner means the new fan-outs can now be picked below.
  4. Under Fan-out Extensions, choose the Fan out, then Extension Cable (NL4/NL8), Extension Length (25'–150') and Quantity.
  5. Under PA Couplers, add NL4, NL8 or CACOM couplers with a quantity. Save.
  6. ✕ removes a row.

Jumpers

  • Set Cable to NL4 Jumper and the length box hides. Jumpers are counted by Count only. They are never cut from stock lengths and add nothing to length totals.
  • Use a cable type instead of a jumper for any run that needs a length.

Good to know

  • Extensions are added to the NL 4 or NL 8 totals. Couplers go in the Couplers column.
