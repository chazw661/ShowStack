Draw a block diagram of your system using the consoles, stage boxes, amps and beltpacks already in this project.

Steps

  1. Open Signal Flow Diagrams in the sidebar, click + New Diagram and give it a name.
  2. Drag a tile from the left onto the canvas: Console, Device, Processor, Amp, Speaker Array, Beltpack or Generic.
  3. In Pick a …, search and click the piece of gear. The shape takes its name. Cancel removes the shape.
  4. Drag shapes to place them. Use −, + and fit to zoom. Hold Space and drag, or drag with the middle mouse button, to pan. ⊞ turns snap-to-grid on and off.
  5. Click a shape to get Re-link equipment and Delete shape. Shift-click or drag a box to select several shapes. Delete/Backspace removes the selection, and ↶/↷ undo and redo.

Good to know

  • The picker only lists gear already in the project. Device means I/O Devices, Processor means System Processors, and Speaker Array comes from Soundvision predictions.
  • Generic is for gear that isn't in ShowStack. Its label stays "Generic", so name it with a text note.
  • Only editors and owners can create and save diagrams.
