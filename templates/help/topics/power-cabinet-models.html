Cabinet models are a shared catalogue of loudspeaker enclosures. Each cabinet lists the sections an amp output can drive, with each section's nominal impedance.

Steps

  1. In the sidebar, tap the ▶ next to Power Distribution Plans, then Cabinet Models, to see the catalogue.
  2. The Sections column shows what each cabinet puts on an amp output. A passive cabinet has one Full range section. An active cabinet has one section per output, for example Kara II has LF and HF.
  3. Tap a cabinet to see where its figures come from, in Source.

Good to know

  • The figures come from the L-Acoustics Preset Guide (v25.0). Most sections are 8 Ω. The 16 Ω and 4 Ω exceptions are listed on page 118 of that guide.
  • A section marked unverified has an impedance the guide doesn't state for that section.
  • It's read-only. Only a ShowStack administrator can add or edit cabinets.
