Send someone an email invite so they can open your project as an editor or a viewer.

Steps

  1. On the Project Dashboard, find your project under Projects You Own and tap Manage Invitations.
  2. Tap + Invite User.
  3. Enter their Email Address, pick a Role (Editor or Viewer; it starts on Viewer) and tap Send Invitation.
  4. They get an email with an Accept Invitation button. If they don't have an account yet, they tap Create Free Account, then sign in and accept.

Good to know

  • Only the project owner can send invitations.
  • They must sign in with the same email address you invited. Signed in with another account, they get an error and are logged out.
  • Invites expire after 7 days. The Manage Invitations page shows each invite's Status and when it was accepted.
  • An invite that hasn't been accepted also appears on their Project Dashboard with an Accept Invitation button.
  • You can't invite someone who is already on the project or already has an invite waiting. There's no button to cancel or resend an invite.
