When one RF mic is handed from presenter to presenter during a session, give it several presenters and step through them.

Steps

  1. On the mic's A2 card press + Add Presenter. A new empty presenter is added and becomes the current one. Type the name.
  2. Repeat for each person who will use the mic.
  3. Use ◀ PREV and NEXT ▶ to move to the presenter who is up. The counter shows which one it is (for example 2 / 3). You can also tap a name chip.
  4. Press ON/OFF on a chip to mark that presenter MIC'D.

Good to know

  • Editors and owners only.
  • Only one presenter per mic can be MIC'D at a time. Turning one on turns the others off.
  • Type, Placement, notes and the photo on the card belong to the current presenter, so each person keeps their own settings.
  • ✕ on a chip removes that presenter from this mic. The last remaining presenter can't be removed.
  • In the A1 list each presenter gets their own row, and the RF# appears only on the first one. Their MIC'D buttons there are display-only.
