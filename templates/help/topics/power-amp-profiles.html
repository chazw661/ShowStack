An amplifier profile holds one amp model's power figures. The power calculator uses them to work out current draw.

Steps

  1. In the sidebar, open Power Distribution Plans, then Amplifier Profiles, and tap Add Amplifier Profile.
  2. Enter Manufacturer, Model, Channels and Rack units.
  3. From the datasheet, enter Idle power watts, Rated power watts (1/8 power, pink noise), Peak power watts (1/3 power) and Max power watts.
  4. Set Nominal voltage and Power factor, then tap Save. It now appears in the Amplifier column of every power plan.

Good to know

  • The calculator uses only idle watts, rated watts, nominal voltage and power factor. Peak, max and efficiency are stored for reference.
  • ShowStack ships with L-Acoustics LA4X, LA8, LA12X and LA7.16i profiles. The LA7.16i profile is set to 7 channels, but the amp has 16, so don't rely on it yet. Check the others against current datasheets.
  • If Amplifier Profiles isn't in your sidebar, your account can't add profiles yet.
  • Profiles are shared by every project and account, so an edit changes them for everyone. Amps already in a plan keep their old current figures until you next change a setting on that plan's calculator page.
