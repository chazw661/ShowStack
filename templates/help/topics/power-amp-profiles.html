Amplifier profiles are a shared catalogue of amp power figures. The power calculator uses them to work out current draw.

Steps

  1. In the sidebar, open Power Distribution Plans, then Amplifier Profiles, to see the catalogue.
  2. Tap a profile to read its Channels, Rack units, power figures, Nominal voltage and Power factor.
  3. To use one, pick it in the Amplifier column of a power plan.

Good to know

  • The catalogue holds the L-Acoustics LA4X, LA8, LA12X and LA7.16i.
  • It's read-only. You can't add or edit profiles yet.
  • Each profile also lists its mains draw per load, as published in the L-Acoustics manual. These figures are at 230 V with all channels driven. Standby, idle, 1/8 and 1/3 power, in amps and watts, with the source page. LA4X figures are unverified.
  • At other voltages, current is the 230 V figure × 230 ÷ the voltage, unless the manual prints its own multiplier (LA8: ×2.0 at 120 V).
  • The calculator doesn't use the per-load figures yet. Until then, check its results against the datasheet before you rely on a plan.
  • The calculator uses only idle and rated (1/8 power) watts, nominal voltage and power factor.
