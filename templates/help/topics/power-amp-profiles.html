Amplifier profiles are a shared catalogue of amp power figures. The power calculator uses them to work out current draw.

Steps

  1. In the sidebar, tap the ▶ next to Power Distribution Plans, then Amplifier Profiles, to see the catalogue.
  2. Tap a profile to read its Channels, Rack units, power figures, and per-load mains draw.
  3. To use one, pick it in the Amplifier column of a power plan.

Good to know

  • The catalogue holds the L-Acoustics LA4X, LA8, LA12X and LA7.16i.
  • It's read-only. You can't add or edit profiles yet.
  • Each profile lists its mains draw per load as published: 230 V, all channels driven, standby to 1/3 power, in amps and watts, with the source page. LA4X figures are unverified.
  • At other voltages, current is the 230 V figure × 230 ÷ the voltage, unless the manual prints its own multiplier (LA8: ×2.0 at 120 V).
  • Power plans use these figures: 1/8 power for normal programme, 1/3 for heavy. With mixed loads, each channel adds its share above idle.
  • A profile without per-load figures falls back to its watts, at the plan's assumed power factor.
