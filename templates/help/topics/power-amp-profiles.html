Amplifier profiles are a shared catalogue of amp power figures. The power calculator uses them to work out current draw.

Steps

  1. In the sidebar, open Power Distribution Plans, then Amplifier Profiles, to see the catalogue.
  2. Tap a profile to read its Channels, Rack units, power figures, Nominal voltage and Power factor.
  3. To use one, pick it in the Amplifier column of a power plan.

Good to know

  • The catalogue holds the L-Acoustics LA4X, LA8, LA12X and LA7.16i.
  • It's read-only. You can't add or edit profiles yet.
  • The figures are estimates. Check them against the manufacturer's datasheet before you rely on a plan.
  • The calculator uses only idle watts, rated (1/8 power) watts, nominal voltage and power factor. Peak, max and efficiency are for reference.
