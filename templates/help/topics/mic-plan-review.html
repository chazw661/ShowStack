The Review tab lists what the showflow left unclear and where the plan is tight. Check each card off once you have dealt with it.

Steps

  1. On the Mic Planner page, open Review. The tab shows unchecked of total, e.g. Review (3 of 8).
  2. Press Checked… on a card, add a note if you like, then Mark checked.
  3. Checked cards move to Checked at the bottom, with who checked them, when and the note. Edit note or Uncheck there.

Good to know

  • Editors and owners only; viewers see checks and notes.
  • A check survives Re-generate, Re-read and Replace. If a card's numbers change (minutes late, RFs, channels), it comes back unchecked.
  • A checked RF conflict, or a shortage while over the rig, still blocks Send to Mic Tracker, and its preview lists it.
  • To review and the dashboard count unchecked cards only.
  • The PDF's review list prints unchecked cards first, then the checked ones.
