One A2's mics and de-mics, by session and segment, in large type. It opens in its own window: a second screen or an iPad.

Steps

  1. On the Mic Plan page, press A2 schedule ↗. With more than one A2, pick yours. A de-mic helper picks Helpers.
  2. Tap a segment as it starts. It stays at the top, the next one with work says NEXT, earlier ones fade. The clock never moves it.
  3. After a re-generate or a move, tap Plan updated — tap to refresh.
  4. PDF prints the schedule, a pack sheet (each RF's wearers in order) and the review list.

Good to know

  • People who only de-mic: set De-mic helpers on a segment heading. They take de-mics in that segment, so packs turn over sooner. Review suggests where one helps.
  • Read-only: moves are made on the Mic Plan page.
  • Marks are words for black-and-white printers: HOST, BACKUP, PINNED, MID-SESSION, CONFLICT, late, tight.
  • Parts that overlap in time are planned as one stage with shared A2s and packs. Plan separate rooms on separate days for now.
