Change the draft plan by moving people between packs. Each move is pinned; the rest of the plan is worked out again around it, and everyone else keeps their RF wherever they can.

Steps

  1. In Pack chart, drag a bar up or down to another RF. On an iPad, tap the bar, then tap a lane. Green lanes are free at that time, red ones are busy. New pack is the dashed lane at the bottom.
  2. Or in Showflow + plan, press a pack chip for Move to RF, Switch body/handheld and Unpin.
  3. Check the changed pill and Review. Undo takes back the last move.

Good to know

  • A move keeps its times and mic type: only Switch changes it.
  • RFs are the rig's channels; either type takes any but SPARE. Another zone's channel or a transmitter swap shows in Review.
  • Red in Review: two people on one RF at once. The plan is kept; fix it with another move.
  • Re-generate keeping my changes replays your moves and keeps RF numbers. Start over clears them and renumbers.
  • After the showflow changes, re-generate before moving packs. A move whose line is gone shows in Review.
  • Viewers see pins, not controls.
