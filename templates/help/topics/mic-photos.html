Put a face on each card so A2s can find the right presenter in a busy green room.

Steps

  1. On the A2 tab, assign a presenter to the card.
  2. Tap the Photo square, or drag an image file (or an image from another web page) onto it.
  3. Choose This person — all sessions or This session only.
  4. Pick the file if asked. The photo appears straight away.

Good to know

  • Editors and owners only. JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB.
  • All sessions follows the person onto every card they are on, and replaces any session-only photos they had.
  • This session only stays on this card and wins over the person's all-sessions photo.
  • Names that look like a mic position (HH13, Q&A 2, A2 OS) are marked as one automatically. A mic position can only have session photos. To change this, open 👤 Presenters, edit the name and untick Mic position — photos stay per session.
  • You can also set or remove an all-sessions photo from the presenter's page (Headshot, Remove headshot).
  • Hover over a photo to enlarge it. Photos also print in Export PDF.
