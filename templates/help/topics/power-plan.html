Estimate the current your amp racks draw on each leg of the power service, and check the legs are balanced.

Steps

  1. Open Power Distribution Plans in the sidebar and tap Add Power Distribution Plan.
  2. Fill in Venue name, Service type, Available amperage per leg, Transient headroom and Safety margin.
  3. Under Amplifiers in Power Plan, add a row per group of amps: Amplifier, Quantity, Zone, Duty cycle, Phase assignment. Tap Save.
  4. In the plans list, tap View Calculator. Changes to Available Amps/Leg, Transient Headroom or Safety Margin there save straight away.

Good to know

  • Current/Unit = (idle watts + 1/8-power watts × duty-cycle %) × headroom ÷ (amp profile voltage × power factor).
  • Usable Amps/Leg = available × safety margin; each leg's % Used is against that.
  • Phase Imbalance = (highest leg − lowest leg) ÷ highest leg. Target under 10%.
  • Auto-Balance deals a row's amps L1, L2, L3 in turn, and every row starts on L1. Many single-amp Auto rows all land on L1, so set those by hand.
  • The plan's voltage is display only; the maths uses each amp profile's voltage.
