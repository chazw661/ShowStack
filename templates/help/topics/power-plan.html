Estimate the current your amp racks draw on each leg of the power service, and check the legs are balanced.

Steps

  1. In the sidebar, tap Power Distribution Plans to open this project's plan.
  2. No plan yet? Tap Generate from amps (owners and editors): a row per amp, on its cabinets, legs balanced.
  3. Under Service Configuration, set the venue, service, amps per leg, headroom, safety margin, power factor and notes. Changes save straight away.
  4. When the amps change, the plan says so: tap Regenerate. Your legs, settings and hand-added rows are kept.

Good to know

  • Every figure is an estimate from manufacturer data: a leg's current is the published watts on it ÷ (phase-to-neutral voltage × power factor), times the headroom. The calculator shows the voltage used, e.g. 120 V on 208Y/120.
  • Normal uses the 1/8-power figure, Heavy the 1/3-power figure. No duty cycle is applied on top.
  • A blank load per channel means the lowest load the amp publishes, which draws the most.
  • The power factor is assumed (0.95 by default).
  • Usable Amps/Leg = available × safety margin. Phase Imbalance = (highest − lowest leg) ÷ highest; target under 10%.
