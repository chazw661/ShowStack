Estimate the current your amp racks draw on each leg of the power service, and check the legs are balanced.

Steps

  1. Open Power Distribution Plans and tap Add Power Distribution Plan.
  2. Fill in the venue, service type, amperage per leg, transient headroom, safety margin and power factor.
  3. Under Amplifiers in Power Plan, add a row per group of amps: amplifier, quantity, zone, Programme, Load per channel, phase. Tap Save.
  4. In the plans list, tap View Calculator. Settings changed there save straight away.

Good to know

  • Every figure is an estimate from manufacturer data: the published draw at 230 V, converted to the plan's voltage, times the headroom.
  • Normal uses the 1/8-power figure, Heavy the 1/3-power figure. No duty cycle is applied on top.
  • A blank load per channel means the lowest load the amp publishes, which draws the most.
  • The power factor is an assumption, used only for amps whose current isn't published.
  • Usable Amps/Leg = available × safety margin. Phase Imbalance = (highest − lowest leg) ÷ highest; target under 10%.
