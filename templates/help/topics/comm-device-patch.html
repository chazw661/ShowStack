Patch a comm device's Dante channels, 4-wire ports and 2-wire ports on its own page, under Comms patch.

Steps

  1. Open Devices in the sidebar and open the comm device.
  2. Dante inputs: for each Dante In, pick the device input the audio goes in at ("MK88 In 3").
  3. Dante outputs: for each Dante Out, pick the device output it comes out of ("MK88 Out 3").
  4. 4-wire ports: pick where Send goes (a device input) and where Receive comes from (a device output). Cable type starts as "4W adapter".
  5. 2-wire ports: type where the port goes ("Wired BP chain SL") and, if you like, a cable type.
  6. Press Save.

Good to know

  • Each box takes an entry exactly as listed; anything else is not saved, and the row says so. 2-wire rows are free text.
  • This device's Dante network is listed first. An entry on another network ends with its name; picking it is allowed, and the Wiring Diagram's Warnings lists it.
  • The device at the other end shows the link under its port ("← Arcadia Dante Out 3"). Click it to open the comm device.
  • The Wiring Diagram's Comms tab draws these links.
