Put a name on each RF mic so the A1 and A2s both know who is wearing what.

Steps

  1. In a session, switch to the A2 tab.
  2. On the mic's card, tap the name field (it reads — Unassigned —).
  3. Pick a name from the list, or keep typing and choose + Add "…" as new presenter.
  4. Set Type, Headset Color, Placement, Sensitivity and Output Level on the same card.

Good to know

  • Editors and owners only.
  • The name saves on its own after a short pause, on Enter, or when you leave the field. Clearing the field unassigns the mic.
  • Names are matched ignoring capitals and extra spaces, so "jane doe" picks up an existing "Jane Doe" instead of making a second one.
  • 👤 Presenters in the header opens the full presenter list, where you can add, rename or delete names and set headshots.
  • In the A1 list you can drag a row onto another row to swap presenters, or to its top or bottom edge to move one. The RF's mic settings stay where they are. This works only on mics with a single presenter.
