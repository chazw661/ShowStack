Name the base station's wired ports and route each one to a partyline, such as a 2-wire to the stage manager desk or a 4-wire to the matrix.

Steps

  1. In a config, open the Ports tab.
  2. Click a port's name (e.g. 2W Port A) to rename it, e.g. "SM Desk".
  3. Pick a partyline from the dropdown next to it.
  4. On Arcadia, choose Talk+Listen, Talk Only or Listen Only.
  5. Set the extras. 2-wire ports have Clear-Com/RTS, Power and Termination. 4-wire ports have 4-Wire-X (To Matrix)/4-Wire (To Panel) and Call Signal, plus Line Level/Mic Level on Arcadia. Every change saves straight away.

Good to know

  • The Arcadia export carries the port name, partyline, Talk/Listen setting and Termination. It does not yet carry the 2-wire mode, Power, the 4-wire function, the level, Call Signal or the Dante Channels list. Set those on the unit.
  • 4W Port 8 on an Arcadia config is not included in the export.
  • The Dante Channels list (+ Add Channel) appears on Arcadia only.
