Print a console's channel list or your stage box patch as a PDF for FOH, monitors or the stage.

Steps

  1. Console: open Consoles and press 📄 PDF in the console's row. The sheet opens in a new tab, ready to print or save.
  2. One device: open I/O Devices and press Export PDF in the device's Actions column. The PDF downloads.
  3. All devices: on I/O Devices, press Export All Devices PDF. Every device in the project goes into one file, in name order.

Good to know

  • The console sheet has Console Inputs, Aux Outputs, Matrix Outputs and Stereo Outputs. Empty rows and empty columns are left out.
  • Only the A side prints: Source (B) and Source Hardware (B) are not on the console sheet.
  • The device sheet lists Input #, Signal and Console Source (console and channel), then Output # and Signal Name. Empty ports are skipped, but the numbers stay true to the box.
  • Console Source is filled in for linked inputs, whether picked on the device page or patched from the console page; typed names leave it blank.
