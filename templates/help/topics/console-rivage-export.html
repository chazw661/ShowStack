Get your channel and mix names onto a Yamaha Rivage PM without typing them on the desk.

Steps

  1. Open Consoles in the sidebar.
  2. In the console's row, press 📊 Yamaha CSV under Exports. (Or tick one console, choose Export Yamaha Rivage CSVs from the Action menu and press Go.)
  3. A zip file named after the console downloads. It holds 11 CSV files, including InName.csv, MixName.csv, MtxName.csv and StName.csv.
  4. Unzip it and import the files into your show in Rivage PM Editor. [CHECK]

Good to know

  • Input names come from Source, placed by Input ch (1–288). A channel with no name, or a non-number in Input ch, comes out as "ch12" and so on.
  • Aux names go to Mix 1–72 and matrix names to Matrix 1–36. Stereo Left/Right go to Stereo A; Mono goes to both sides of Stereo B.
  • Only names travel. Inputs come in blue and mixes orange, DCA and mute names are generic, and the patch files are blank. Source (B), groups, DCAs, mutes and Dante numbers are not exported.
  • Commas in names become semicolons.
  • The files are written in the Rivage V6.60 format. [CHECK]
