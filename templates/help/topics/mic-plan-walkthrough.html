The Walkthrough tab shows each showflow line as ShowStack read it: the time, who comes on and off, and their mic type. Confirm each line, or fix it there.

Steps

  1. Open Walkthrough. It starts on the first line no one has confirmed.
  2. Press Enter to confirm a line and move on; ↑ ↓ move without confirming.
  3. A locked line needs a decision first: Person, Not a person or Same as… for a new name, or Looks right for an exit, a line split across pages, an inferred mic or text that is not a person.
  4. Edit takes someone off a line, adds someone, or changes their mic. Saving confirms the line.

Good to know

  • Green words were read as a name; dashed ones were not. A dashed chip is an assumed off.
  • If the showflow changes under a confirmed line, it reopens and shows what changed. Re-generate reopens nothing.
  • Plan issues show as labels on their line. Show in plan opens that line with its RFs.
  • RF conflicts, a rig shortage and held-back changes block Send to Mic Tracker even on confirmed lines.
  • Stat tiles say unverified until every line is confirmed.
