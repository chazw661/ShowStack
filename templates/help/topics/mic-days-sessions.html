Build the show schedule in the Mic Tracker: one show day per date, and sessions inside each day, each with its own set of RF mics.

Steps

  1. Open the Mic Tracker card on the dashboard.
  2. Press + Add Day, pick the Date, add an optional Name / Label, then Add Day.
  3. Under that day press + Add Session. Enter the Session Name, the Number of Mics (1–100) and an optional Location / Ballroom, then Add Session. RF 1 up to that number are created.
  4. To copy a session, press 📋 (Duplicate) in its header and type a name. The copy lands in the same day.
  5. For times, type, highlight colour or mic count, press ⚙ (Settings).

Good to know

  • Editors and owners only.
  • Only one day per date. A second day on the same date is refused.
  • A duplicate copies presenters, mic settings, notes, photos and the MIC'D state. Colour groups, headset colour and Audio Ch are not copied.
  • To delete a session, open ⚙, press Delete, then type the session name exactly. Delete stays locked until it matches.
