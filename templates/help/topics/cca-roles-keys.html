Make a role for each crew position and decide what every key on that beltpack or panel talks and listens to.

Steps

  1. In a config, open the Roles tab and click + Add Role.
  2. Enter a Role Name (e.g. "A2"), pick a Device Type and click Add Role.
  3. Click the role's row to open its keys.
  4. For each key, choose a partyline, an activation (Talk + FL, Talk + Listen, Talk Only or Listen Only) and a talk mode (Latching, Momentary or Disabled). Each change saves straight away.

Good to know

  • Key counts follow the device. A FreeSpeak II beltpack gets keys A–E and an Edge beltpack gets A–I. In both, the last key is the reply key. V-Panels number their keys 1–12, 1–24 or 1–32.
  • FreeSpeak II configs only offer the two FreeSpeak beltpacks. Arcadia also offers HelixNet devices and V-Panels.
  • The role name field suggests names from Crew Names. A name you type that isn't on that list gets added to it.
  • Keep role names to 20 characters or fewer.
  • Delete Role removes the role and all its keys.
