Mark each mic MIC'D as it goes on, using the A1 list at FOH or the A2 cards backstage.

Steps

  1. In a session, pick A1 for a compact list (RF#, Type, Presenter, MIC'D, A1 Notes) or A2 for full cards with photo and mic settings.
  2. Press the OFF button to turn it ON (green) when the mic is on the presenter. Press it again to clear it.
  3. To colour-code mics, press GRP, add a group name and colour, then tap the dot at the left of an A1 row (or on an A2 card) to assign it.
  4. For the whole show at a glance, open the Overview tab. It lists every day and session and lets you change Type, Headset, Placement and Mic'd.

Good to know

  • Changing MIC'D and groups is for editors and owners only. Viewers see the buttons locked.
  • Each session remembers its A1/A2 choice on this device.
  • The bar under each session counts MIC'D and D-MIC mics. Each day header shows the totals for that day.
  • A1 and A2 groups are separate, so the A1 and A2s can each colour mics their own way.
